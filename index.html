About the conference

A meeting point for two fields that need each other

The conference aims to bring together researchers, academicians, industry experts, and students to explore advancements in quantum computing, quantum machine learning, artificial intelligence, quantum networking, and quantum-safe security. It will cover key concepts, emerging applications, and challenges in quantum algorithms, quantum communication, error correction, and post-quantum cryptography, with a focus on secure, scalable, and interdisciplinary innovations.

The three-day programme will feature keynote addresses, expert talks, technical paper presentations, panel discussions, and collaborative sessions, fostering knowledge exchange, research innovation, and academic–industry collaborations in next-generation quantum technologies.

QCAI 2027 brings researchers, engineers and students together in New Delhi to present recent advances in quantum machine learning, quantum algorithms, quantum communication and measurement, quantum security, and the engineering problems that sit between them. The program includes invited keynotes, oral paper sessions, a poster session and an industry panel.

Selected high-quality papers presented at QCAI 2027 may be invited for further publication in reputed journals, subject to the journals’ scope and standard peer-review process. Special issues may also be arranged with relevant journals, subject to editorial approval.

All accepted and presented papers will be published in the conference proceedings.

    For authors

    Submission & Publication

    How to submit

    1. Download the paper template (Word or LaTeX) from the Downloads section.
    2. Prepare your manuscript as a PDF. Remove author names and affiliations for double-blind review.
    3. Open the submission form and enter the title, abstract, keywords, track and all author details.
    4. Upload your PDF in the form. File uploads in Google Forms require signing in with a Google account.
    5. You will receive a confirmation email. Contact inconfqcai@gmail.com if it does not arrive within 48 hours.

    Review process

    Every submission is checked for scope and similarity, then reviewed double-blind by at least two members of the Technical Program Committee. Decisions are sent by email on the notification date.

    Publication

    Accepted and registered papers presented at the conference will be published in the conference proceedings and submitted for indexing. At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present the paper.

    For authors

    Editorial Policy

    Originality

    Submissions must be original work that has not been published and is not under review elsewhere. Every manuscript is screened with plagiarism-detection software. Papers with a similarity index above 20%, or with copied text, figures or data, are rejected without review.

    Peer review

    We use double-blind review. Reviewers do not know the authors' identities and authors do not know the reviewers'. Reviewers are asked to declare any conflict of interest and to decline papers they cannot assess fairly.

    Authorship

    Everyone listed as an author must have made a substantial contribution to the work and must approve the final version. Changes to the author list after acceptance need written approval from all authors.

    Conflicts of interest

    Authors must disclose funding sources and any financial or personal relationship that could be seen as influencing the work.

    Research ethics

    Data must be reported accurately. Fabrication, falsification and selective reporting are grounds for rejection or, after publication, retraction.

    Corrections and retractions

    If an author finds a significant error in a published paper, they should contact the organisers promptly so a correction or retraction can be issued.

    For authors

    Guidelines for AI Tools

    How authors and reviewers may use generative AI tools such as large language models.

    For authors

    • AI tools cannot be listed as authors. Authors are fully responsible for the content of their paper.
    • Using AI for language editing or formatting is allowed and does not need to be declared.
    • Any other use, such as drafting text, generating code, analysing data or creating figures, must be described in the Methods or Acknowledgements section, with the tool name and version.
    • AI-generated images or figures that present experimental results are not allowed.
    • Authors must check every reference and fact produced with the help of an AI tool.

    For reviewers

    • Manuscripts under review are confidential. Do not upload them, in full or in part, to any AI tool.
    • Reviewers may use AI to improve the language of their own review, but the assessment must be their own.
    • Report any suspected undisclosed AI use to the Program Chairs.

      Registration guidance for authors, presenters and attendees at QCAI 2027.

      Registration process

      Follow the official QCAI 2027 announcements and instructions throughout the registration process.

      • Check the email address used for your CMT submission for an acceptance decision.
      • Review the reviewers’ comments and use them to improve your paper.
      • Prepare the final camera-ready paper according to QCAI’s author instructions. Verify author details, affiliations, figures, references and other manuscript content before submitting.
      • When QCAI publishes its registration categories and payment instructions, follow the instructions for your category and retain your payment confirmation.
      • Complete the official registration form when it becomes available. Check your name, contact information, paper ID and payment details before submitting.
      • Watch your email and the conference website for registration confirmation and the presentation schedule.
